# Generated image provenance

아래 배경과 편집 이미지는 2026-08-20~21에 Codex 내장 ImageGen으로 생성했다. 로고,
브랜드명, 오류 번호와 UI 문구는 생성 이미지에 포함하지 않고 코드 또는 canonical SVG로
합성한다.

## `public/media/404-signal.webp`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: responsive website 404 page background plate
Primary request: an abstract “lost broadcast signal” composition for a minimalist Korean news brand
Scene/backdrop: deep matte black field with sparse torn newsprint fibers, faint analog scan lines,
photocopy dust, broken transmission arcs, and one very small signal-red registration dot
Style/medium: premium editorial xerox and halftone collage, restrained, tactile, nocturnal,
not photorealistic
Composition/framing: square center-safe composition that can crop to both wide desktop and 4:5
mobile; preserve generous quiet negative space in the center for an existing logo and 404 copy overlay;
visual activity should live mostly around the outer edges
Color palette: black, warm off-white, charcoal gray, a single tiny #ff3b30 accent
Constraints: background plate only; no rabbit, no mascot, no people, no faces, no objects suggesting
a real news event, no legible newspaper copy, no text, no numbers, no logos, no watermark
Avoid: cyberpunk neon, colorful glitch effects, busy collage, fake headlines, stock-photo look
```

## `public/media/hero-newsroom.webp`

2026-08-21에 생성했다. `hero-newsroom-mobile.webp`는 같은 원본의 중심 수신 장치를
보존한 3:4 반응형 크롭이고, `assets/og-background-v2.webp`는 공유 카드용 1200×630
크롭이다.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-bleed responsive homepage hero image for a premium independent short-form news brand
Primary request: create a cinematic nocturnal editorial newsroom tableau that feels like information
being received, verified, and transmitted
Scene/backdrop: a deep matte-black studio/archive space with one sculptural analog broadcast receiver
made of translucent smoked glass, layered blank newsprint sheets, a metal contact-sheet frame, subtle
projection beams, floating dust, and a single tiny red tally light
Subject: abstract editorial objects only; the receiver and paper layers form a strong central-right
focal sculpture, never a literal newsroom desk
Style/medium: ultra-premium art-directed editorial photography blended with subtle photogram and
darkroom texture; tactile, restrained, contemporary, museum-catalog sophistication
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 master image; dramatic asymmetrical composition; keep the
lower-left and left-center relatively calm and dark for large HTML headline typography; center-safe
focal object so the image can crop gracefully to portrait mobile; layered depth from foreground paper
edge to distant haze
Lighting/mood: low-key directional light, soft warm-white highlights, rich blacks with readable shadow
detail, one minimal signal-red glow, quiet authority rather than thriller drama
Color palette: near-black, charcoal, warm ivory, smoked silver, one tiny #ff3b30 accent
Materials/textures: real paper fibers, brushed metal, smoked glass, subtle film grain, faint scan-line
interference
Constraints: no people, no faces, no hands, no identifiable real place, no readable document content,
no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no rabbit, no watermark; clearly art-directed abstraction,
not documentary evidence
Avoid: cyberpunk neon, blue-purple RGB glow, fake headlines, computer UI, stock-photo newsroom, crime
scene, police imagery, weapons, blood, sensationalism, clutter
```
