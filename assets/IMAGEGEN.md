# Generated image provenance

아래 배경과 편집 이미지는 2026-08-20에 Codex 내장 ImageGen으로 생성했다. 로고, 브랜드명,
오류 번호와 UI 문구는 생성 이미지에 포함하지 않고 코드 또는 canonical SVG로 합성한다.

## `public/media/404-signal.webp`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: responsive website 404 page background plate
Primary request: an abstract “lost broadcast signal” composition for a minimalist Korean news brand
Scene/backdrop: deep matte black field with sparse torn newsprint fibers, faint analog scan lines,
photocopy dust, broken transmission arcs, and one very small signal-red registration dot
Style/medium: premium editorial xerox and halftone collage, restrained, tactile, nocturnal,
not photorealistic
Composition/framing: square center-safe composition that can crop to both wide desktop and 4:5
mobile; preserve generous quiet negative space in the center for an existing logo and 404 copy overlay;
visual activity should live mostly around the outer edges
Color palette: black, warm off-white, charcoal gray, a single tiny #ff3b30 accent
Constraints: background plate only; no rabbit, no mascot, no people, no faces, no objects suggesting
a real news event, no legible newspaper copy, no text, no numbers, no logos, no watermark
Avoid: cyberpunk neon, colorful glitch effects, busy collage, fake headlines, stock-photo look
```

## `public/media/report-evidence.webp`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide editorial section image for an anonymous news tip submission area
Primary request: a quiet top-down evidence-review still life that suggests receiving photos, video,
documents, and links without depicting any real event
Scene/backdrop: matte black editorial desk with a blank unbranded smartphone, a small memory card,
layered off-white document sheets with abstract black redaction bars, a translucent contact sheet with
nonfigurative frames, and one thin signal-red registration thread
Style/medium: tactile premium editorial photography mixed with subtle xerox grain; restrained,
believable materials, deliberately staged still life rather than photojournalism
Composition/framing: very wide 16:7 horizontal strip; objects distributed across the middle band with
calm dark negative space around them; crop-safe on mobile
Lighting/mood: soft directional studio light, deep blacks, quiet investigative mood
Color palette: black, warm off-white, charcoal, one minimal #ff3b30 accent
Materials/textures: real paper fibers, matte glass, worn card plastic, subtle dust and film grain
Constraints: no people, no hands, no faces, no identifiable places, no logos, no brands, no readable
document text, no headlines, no UI text, no watermark
Avoid: crime-scene imagery, sensationalism, police props, weapons, blood, fake news photography,
glossy stock-photo look, colorful screens
```
